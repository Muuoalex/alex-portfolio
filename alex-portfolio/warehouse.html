CIVIL ENGINEERING PROJECT - ( ACADEMIC PROJECT )

Warehouse Design & Construction

📍 Thika, Kiambu County, Kenya

Proposed Design and Construction of a Warehouse Building for Storage and Distribution of Agricultural Products.

🏗️Project Overview

This project presents a proposed warehouse facility designed for the storage and distribution of agricultural products in Thika, Kiambu County, Kenya.

The project covers building design, structural considerations, drainage and sanitation, water supply and reticulation, architectural drawings, estimating and construction planning.

📐 Project Components

  • Architectural Design

    • Building arrangement, storage areas, loading bay, offices, washrooms and circulation areas.

  • Structural Design

    • Structural elements including foundations, reinforced concrete columns and beams, and steel roof trusses.

  • Water Supply

    • Water supply and reticulation system incorporating storage and distribution.

  • Drainage & Sanitation

    • Drainage pipes, inspection chambers, septic tank, soak pit and surface-water drainage.

  • Estimating & Costing

    • Preparation of project quantities and a Bill of Quantities for the proposed works.

  • Construction Planning

    • Proposed construction programme covering site clearance, foundations, walls, roofing, finishes, services and external works.

    👷 My Role in the Project

    📐 Design

    Participated in the planning and development of the proposed warehouse design.

    📊 Estimating

    Worked on quantity take-offs, estimating and preparation of the Bill of Quantities.

    🏗️ Construction Planning

    Location: Thika, Kiambu County, Kenya
